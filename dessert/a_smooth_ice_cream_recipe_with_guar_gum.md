# A Smooth Ice Cream Recipe with Guar Gum

## Ingredients:

- 4.7 dl heavy cream, cold
- 2.4 dl whole milk
- 150 g granulated sugar
- 1/4 tsp guar gum
- 5 large egg yolks
- 1/8 tsp fine sea salt
- 15 ml vanilla extract or 1 vanilla bean, split and scraped

## TODO:

1. Before you begin, place the freezer bowl of your ice cream maker into the coldest part of your freezer for at least 24 hours. Prepare an ice bath by filling a large bowl with ice and a little water, and set a smaller bowl inside it.
2. In a small, separate bowl, whisk together the granulated sugar and the guar gum. This is the most important step! Mixing the guar gum with sugar first prevents it from clumping when it hits the liquid.
3. In a medium saucepan, warm the heavy cream and whole milk over medium heat until it's steamy and small bubbles form around the edges. If using a vanilla bean, add the pod and scrapings now. Do not let it boil.
4. While the milk is warming, whisk the egg yolks in a medium bowl until they are pale and smooth. Gradually pour in the sugar/guar gum mixture and whisk until fully combined.
5. Slowly, and while whisking constantly, pour about one cup of the hot milk mixture into the egg yolk mixture. This is called tempering and prevents the eggs from scrambling. Pour the tempered egg mixture back into the saucepan with the remaining milk.
6. Cook the custard over medium-low heat, stirring constantly with a wooden spoon or spatula, until it thickens enough to coat the back of the spoon (this is called "nappe"). This should take about 6-8 minutes. The temperature should be around 75-80°C (167-176°F) (77-79°C (171-174°F)). Do not let it curdle.
7. Immediately pour the custard through a fine-mesh sieve into the small bowl set in your ice bath. This catches any bits of cooked egg and the vanilla pod. Stir in the vanilla extract (if using) and the salt. Let it cool in the ice bath for about 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
8. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap, pressing it directly onto the surface of the custard to prevent a skin from forming. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours, but preferably overnight. The base must be completely cold before churning.
9. Pour the cold custard base into your frozen ice cream maker bowl and churn according to the manufacturer's instructions, typically for 20-25 minutes, or until it reaches the consistency of soft-serve.
10. Transfer the churned ice cream to a freezer-safe, airtight container. Press a piece of parchment paper or plastic wrap directly on the surface. Freeze for at least 4 hours to "ripen" and harden to a scoopable consistency.
