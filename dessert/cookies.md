# ENGLISH TEA COOKIES
## Ingredients
- 250 g plain/all-purpose flour
- 160 ml / 1.6 dl milk
- 115 g unsalted butter, chilled and diced
- 50 g granulated sugar
- 3 g salt
- 12 g baking powder

* Preheat oven to 220 °C conventional / 200 °C fan.
* In a large bowl, combine 250 g flour, 12 g baking powder, 3 g salt, and 50 g sugar.
* Cut in 115 g chilled butter using a pastry cutter until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
* Gradually stir in 160 ml milk until the dough pulls away from the sides of the bowl.
* Roll out dough on a floured surface and cut out biscuits using a round cutter.
* Place biscuits on a baking sheet and bake for 13–15 minutes, or until golden brown.
