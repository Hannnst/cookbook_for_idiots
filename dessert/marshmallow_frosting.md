# Marshmallow Frosting (italian meringue)

## Ingredients:

- 4 large egg whites
- 200 g sugar
- 60 ml water
- 1 ml cream of tartar, lemon juice or vinegar
- 5 ml pure vanilla extract

## TODO:

1. Combine sugar and water in a small saucepan. Heat over medium, stirring just until the sugar dissolves. Once boiling, stop stirring and cook until the syrup reaches 115–117°C. Remove from heat.
2. In the clean bowl of an electric mixer fitted with the whisk attachment, beat egg whites and cream of tartar, starting on low speed and gradually increasing to medium-high, until soft peaks form.
3. With the mixer running on medium-high, slowly stream the hot syrup into the egg whites in a thin, steady stream, avoiding the whisk.
4. Increase to high speed and beat until stiff, glossy peaks form and the bowl feels cool, about 5 to 7 minutes.
5. Add vanilla, and mix until combined. Use immediately or freeze.
