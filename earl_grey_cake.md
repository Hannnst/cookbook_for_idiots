# Earl Grey Tea Cake

## Ingredients:

### Infusions:
- 4 Earl Grey tea bags
- 2 tbsp Earl Grey loose leaf tea, finely ground
- 1 dl whole milk
- 100 g unsalted butter

### Cake:
- 150 g all-purpose flour
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- ½ tsp salt
- 100 g granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
  `The rest of the infused milk and butter above goes into the batter.`

### Frosting:
- 200 g cream cheese, softened
- 50 g unsalted butter, softened
- 150 g powdered sugar, sifted
- 1 Earl Grey tea bag, contents finely ground
- 1 tbsp heavy cream
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

## TODO:
1. Warm the milk in a small saucepan until it just starts to steam. Take it off the heat, drop in 2 tea bags, and steep for 10 minutes. Squeeze the bags against the side of the pan, then throw them away.
2. Let the milk cool to room temperature.
3. Melt the 100 g butter over low heat, stir in the ground loose leaf tea, and let it sit off the heat for 15 minutes. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve so no gritty bits remain.
   **Tip:** Both infusions can be made up to 2 days ahead. Keep the milk in the fridge, the butter at room temperature.
4. Cream the tea-infused butter and sugar with an electric mixer until light and fluffy, about 3-4 minutes.
5. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla and the cooled tea-infused milk.
6. Whisk the flour, baking powder, and salt in a separate bowl. Add to the wet mixture in three additions, folding gently with a rubber spatula. Stop as soon as the flour disappears.
7. Preheat the oven to 165°C (330°F). Grease an 8-inch (20 cm) round cake pan and line the bottom with parchment paper.
8. Pour in the batter and smooth the top. Bake for 40-45 minutes, until a toothpick in the center comes out with a few moist crumbs. Do not overbake.
9. Let cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a cooling rack and cool completely.

### Frosting:
10. Beat the cream cheese and butter until completely smooth.
11. Add the powdered sugar gradually, beating until fluffy. Then mix in the heavy cream, ground tea, vanilla, and salt. Beat for 2 minutes more, until spreadable. Visible tea specks are a feature, not a bug.
12. Spread the frosting over the cooled cake. Swirls or smooth, whatever you like. Dust with a little extra ground tea if you want.
13. Let the finished cake rest for 30 minutes before serving.

### Serving and Storage:
- Plain cake keeps 2-5 days in an airtight container at room temperature. With this frosting, refrigerate and eat within 4-5 days.
- Both can be frozen: cake for up to 2 months, frosting up to 1 month. Thaw the cake in the fridge overnight.
- Cake layers baked 1-2 days ahead actually taste better. Wrap them tightly and keep at room temperature.
- Serve with a pot of Earl Grey, fresh berries, or a spoonful of lemon curd. The citrus in the curd plays nicely with the bergamot.
- Before serving a refrigerated cake, let it come to room temperature so the bergamot comes back to life.
